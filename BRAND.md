> Get the gist. Skip the noise.

---

## Theme

**Style:** Clean, minimal, confident — like a well-designed morning briefing.

**Typography:** Lowercase wordmark in medium-weight sans-serif (Inter or DM Sans). "gist" in Ink, "io" in Signal Blue.

**Visuals:**
- Dark Ink header band on the digest email
- White card body, Parchment background
- Orange left-border on "Needs Attention" items
- Subtle colored pills for email categories
- No decoration for decoration's sake

**Logo mark:** Stylized "G" with a horizontal stroke through it — evoking a bullet point, the act of extracting the essential point.

## Brand Dictionary

Consistent language used across the UI, digest email, docs, and marketing.

| Concept | Use | Avoid |
|---|---|---|
| Running the digest | "digest run" | sweep, scan, sync |
| Emails kept unread | "Needs attention" | Important, Unread, Flagged |
| Emails cleaned up | "Cleared" | Archived, Deleted, Processed |
| The email Gistio sends | "Digest" | Summary, Report, Newsletter |
| The inbox cleanup action | "Clear" (verb) | Archive, Mark read, Dismiss |
| The product | "Gistio" | the tool, the script, Gmail Triage |
| Core value prop | "Get the gist" | Triage, Filter, Sort |
| The AI behind it | "AI" | Gemini, LLM, model |

Keeping AI provider-agnostic ("AI" not "Gemini") ensures the brand works if the underlying model ever changes.

---

## Voice & Tone

- **Confident, not boastful** — "Your inbox, distilled." not "The world's best email AI."
- **Minimal, not sparse** — every word earns its place
- **Helpful, not assistive** — Gistio acts; it doesn't ask permission
- **No jargon** — "cleans your inbox" not "optimizes email triage workflows"
