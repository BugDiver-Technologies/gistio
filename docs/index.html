How it works

Five steps. Zero inbox anxiety.

1

Fetch

Reads your unread inbox threads — up to 100 at a time.

2

Classify

AI scores each thread by category and priority in seconds.

3

Clear

Low-priority threads are marked as read or archived for you.

4

Label

Processed threads are labelled so they're skipped next run.

5

Digest

A clean summary email lands in your inbox — what needs you, what was cleared.

Features

Everything you need. Nothing you don't.

AI triage

Every email classified by category and priority. High-priority threads stay unread so you never miss what matters.

Inbox cleanup

Mark low-priority threads as read or archive them entirely. Your choice, your inbox.

Flexible schedule

Run hourly, daily at your chosen time, or monthly. Set it and forget it.

Run on demand

Need it now? Trigger a digest run instantly from the Gmail sidebar.

Styled digest email

Color-coded by category, clickable thread links, and a clear "Needs Attention" section.

Privacy-first

No external servers. Everything runs inside Google Apps Script. Your data stays yours.

How Gistio stacks up.

Three different ways to get AI on top of your inbox — each built for a different job. Here's honestly where each one fits, Gistio included.

  Gistio MorningBrief Google Workspace Flows
Cleans your inbox Marks low-priority mail read or archives it automatically Read-only — summarizes, but your inbox is untouched Depends what the flow is built to do — not inbox-cleanup by default
Who can turn it on Any individual — install from the Marketplace, no admin needed Any individual — sign up directly Needs an org admin to build and enable the flow first
Where you use it Native Gmail Add-on sidebar, plus a digest email Digest email only, separate web dashboard A separate Flows/AppSheet builder and run-history UI

Setup effort A few minutes — install, paste a Gemini key, pick a schedule Sign up and go — nothing to configure Most effort — someone has to design the flow itself
Customization Open source — schedule, label, archive vs. mark-read, all yours to tune or fork Whatever the product exposes in settings Very flexible — can chain into Chat, Sheets, other apps beyond email
Data path Stays inside Google Apps Script end to end, your own API key Emails sent to a third-party service for processing Stays inside Google Workspace infrastructure
Where Gistio asks more of you: you'll need to grab a free Gemini API key yourself and paste it in during setup — it's the trade-off for not depending on Google's managed billing or an org admin. It's a two-minute step, but it's a step MorningBrief and an org-provisioned Flow don't require of you.

Privacy

Your emails never leave Google.

Gistio runs entirely on Google Apps Script. Your API key is stored in your personal Script Properties. No tracking. No ads. No third-party servers.
