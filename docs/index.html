Free  ·  Runs inside Google  ·  No data leaves your account

How it works

Five steps. Zero inbox anxiety.

1

Fetch

Reads your unread inbox threads — up to 100 at a time.

2

Classify

AI scores each thread by category and priority in seconds.

3

Clear

Low-priority threads are marked as read or archived for you.

4

Label

Processed threads are labelled so they're skipped next run.

5

Digest

A clean summary email lands in your inbox — what needs you, what was cleared.

Features

Everything you need. Nothing you don't.

⚡

AI triage

Every email classified by category and priority. High-priority threads stay unread so you never miss what matters.

✓

Inbox cleanup

Mark low-priority threads as read or archive them entirely. Your choice, your inbox.

📅

Flexible schedule

Run hourly, daily at your chosen time, or monthly. Set it and forget it.

▶

Run on demand

Need it now? Trigger a digest run instantly from the Gmail sidebar.

📩

Styled digest email

Color-coded by category, clickable thread links, and a clear "Needs Attention" section.

🔒

Privacy-first

No external servers. Everything runs inside Google Apps Script. Your data stays yours.

Privacy

Your emails never leave Google.

Gistio runs entirely on Google Apps Script. Your API key is stored in your personal Script Properties. No tracking. No ads. No third-party servers.
